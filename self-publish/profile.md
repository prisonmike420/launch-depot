## What it is
Launch Depot is a reusable agent workflow for researching, prioritizing, preparing, submitting, and verifying a real product or website across relevant launch directories, Agent Skill registries, design galleries, company databases, and editorial discovery channels.

The project deliberately uses a relevance-first model rather than bulk submission. It separates automated form submissions, editorial/community channels, paid placements, reciprocal-badge requirements, and human approval boundaries.
