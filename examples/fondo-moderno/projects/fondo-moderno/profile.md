## What it is
Fondo Moderno is an independent platform documenting, preserving and reactivating modern architecture, beginning in Mexico City.

The live product currently includes:
- a growing index of modernist buildings;
- architect pages;
- curated architectural walks/guides, city by city;
- researched color systems connected to the buildings that use them.

The homepage also states that Fondo Moderno documents buildings and the lives around them, creates architectural routes, and opens selected modernist homes for temporary stays.
