## Long description
Fondo Moderno is an independent platform dedicated to documenting, preserving and reactivating modern architecture. It brings together a growing index of buildings and architects, curated architectural walks organized city by city, and research into color systems traced through the buildings that use them. The project begins in Mexico City and treats modern architecture as something to research, visit and experience in place.
