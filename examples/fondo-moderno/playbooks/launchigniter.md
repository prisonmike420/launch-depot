# LaunchIgniter
Source: https://launchigniter.com/about
Checked: 2026-09-25

Fit: good. Public categories include Design Resources, Maps, Travel, Interior design, Web App and more.

Workflow:
1. Search existing listing.
2. Submit product details and current screenshots.
3. Prefer the most accurate non-SaaS category.
4. Choose free/basic route unless a human approves paid promotion.
5. Verify public listing and outbound link after publication.
