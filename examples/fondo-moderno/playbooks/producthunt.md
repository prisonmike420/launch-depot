# Product Hunt
Source: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch
Checked: 2026-09-25

Fit: strong digital-product fit.

Prepare:
- maker identity approved by human;
- canonical URL;
- tagline <= current form limit (observed 60 chars; recheck);
- description <= current form limit (observed 500 chars; recheck);
- square thumbnail (PH recommends 240×240);
- 3–5 real product images.

Workflow:
1. Search Product Hunt + My Products for existing listing.
2. Start New Product with canonical URL.
3. Fill only factual product fields.
4. Choose accurate tags/categories.
5. Upload current screenshots.
6. Attach approved maker.
7. Stop before final scheduling if launch date has not been approved.
8. Record `scheduled` only after date/timezone is confirmed.
9. On launch day, verify public page logged out before `live`.

Never solicit votes.
