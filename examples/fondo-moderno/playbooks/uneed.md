# Uneed
Source: https://www.uneed.best/how-it-works
Checked: 2026-09-25

Free route currently assigns a launch date roughly 30 days–5 months out.
Free launch retention/backlink treatment depends on launch score.

Workflow:
1. Search first.
2. Submit URL and review imported data.
3. Use factual digital-product framing.
4. Choose free waiting line by default.
5. Stop if paid Fast-track / Skip-the-Line is the only available route.
6. Record scheduled date.
7. After launch, record public URL and actual link attribute.
